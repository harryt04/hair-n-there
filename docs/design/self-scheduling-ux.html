Recommended for phones

B · Week strip + time chips

A swipeable row of days, each showing how many times are open, with times grouped below by Morning/Afternoon/Evening. The calendar button opens the full month as a bottom Sheet on phones and a Popover on desktop.

Why
  • Days and times share one screen, so there’s little scrolling between choices.
  • The salon-app convention customers already know.
  • The “4 open / Full” label on each day saves dead-end taps.
  • The strip runs edge to edge, so the partly visible next day signals that it scrolls.
Cost
  • Dates more than about a week out are one tap away (the calendar sheet).
  • The strip is custom UI on top of shadcn Button; it needs careful focus handling and labels.
New · Calendly-style

D · Drill-in: date, then times

A full-width month calendar. Tapping a day replaces it with that day’s times as a full-width list, with “← Dates” to go back. On desktop it falls back to option A’s side-by-side layout.

Why
  • Each view does one job, so there’s no long scroll.
  • Big full-width time rows are the easiest targets.
  • Uses the stock shadcn Calendar.
Cost
  • Comparing days means going back and forth.
  • The view swap needs focus management, moving focus to the new heading.
Best on desktop

A · Month calendar + time list

The classic shadcn booking block: Calendar beside the times on desktop. On phones the times stack under the calendar, and the page scrolls down to them after a day is picked.

Why
  • The whole month is visible at once: full days are faded and open days are dotted.
  • Least custom code.
Cost on phones
  • The calendar takes about 380px, so the times start below the fold and we auto-scroll down to them.
  • Going back to change the day means scrolling up.
Fewest taps

C · Soonest openings first

The next three openings appear as a RadioGroup, with “See all dates & times” expanding a calendar underneath. This could also sit on top of B or D.

Why
  • One tap for “whenever’s soonest”.
  • Fills earlier gaps in her calendar.
Cost
  • Can feel pushy, and anyone with a particular day in mind gets an extra step.
  • On a phone the expanded calendar makes this the longest page.

Interaction spec for implementers

This is the checklist the UI agent and the reviewer will validate against. Mobile rules come first.

Mobile (primary)

  • Design at 360–390px first; desktop is an enhancement through container/breakpoint rules.
  • Targets: days and nav at least 44px; time buttons at least 52px tall, in 2 columns (B/A) or full-width rows (D).
  • Once a time is chosen, a sticky bottom bar shows “Wed, Nov 18 · 10:00 AM” and Continue, with safe-area padding. It must never cover the field being typed in: hide it while the address input is focused.
  • The month calendar opens as a bottom Sheet on phones, not a popover.
  • After choosing a day, keep the times in view (scroll into view, or drill in), allowing for the 104px sticky app bar and ribbon (scroll-margin-top).
  • Inputs use 16px text (no iOS zoom), and the address uses autocomplete="street-address".
  • No horizontal page scroll at 360px; only the week strip scrolls sideways, edge to edge, with the next day partly visible.

Calendar

  • shadcn Calendar, mode="single"; a fluid 7-column grid on phones ([--cell-size:--spacing(11)], minimum 44px) and fixed 40px cells in the desktop split.
  • Disable dates outside the first/last bookable dates and dates with 0 slots; don’t hide them.
  • Optional gold “has openings” dot via a modifiers class.
  • Month navigation refetches; “previous” is disabled before the first bookable month. Opens on the first date with slots.

Times

  • A RadioGroup of button-styled radios: arrow keys move between times and Tab leaves the group.
  • Label: start time large, end time small (“10:00 AM / until 12:30 PM”).
  • Grouped Morning (<12), Afternoon (12–5), Evening (≥5) when there are more than 6 slots.
  • “Times are in Utah (Mountain Time)” is always visible, plus a hint if the visitor’s time zone is different.
  • The buffer is private: never show it, and never show busy reasons.

After choosing

  • A summary appears: day, time range, “Tentative hold”, “Change”, using aria-live="polite".
  • Copy: “Elisabeth will confirm or suggest another time.” Never “Booked” or “Confirmed”.
  • Stage 3 email becomes required, with the hint “Your calendar invitation goes here.”

Fallbacks

  • “None of these work? Describe your timing instead” switches back to today’s free-text fields and clears the slot.
  • Consultation needed, too long, or live times unavailable: show the free-text fields with a short note.
  • Slot taken (409): go back to stage 2 with an alert, clear the slot, refetch, and keep the date.
  • Loading uses skeletons sized like the final layout so nothing jumps.

Accessibility & order

  • A fieldset/legend “Choose a time”; errors linked with aria-describedby; disabled days announce “No times available”.
  • Drill-in and sheet views move focus to their heading and return it on close.
  • Order: estimate line, picker, summary, address, then Back/Continue. Continue isn’t blocked without a slot (that means a flexible inquiry, as today).
