# Portfolio sources

Professional hair work by Elisabeth Muir, collected from her public prior Instagram account, @justbeyou_hair. Harry authorized reuse on the HairnThere website because Elisabeth cannot access the original account email.

Filenames contain the original post shortcode followed by the one-based asset position. Source post URLs, original captions, dates, and ordered media references are in `src/data/instagram-posts.json`; collection limits are in `docs/instagram-inventory.md`.

These are locally optimized copies of publicly available Instagram images. Reel assets are cover images, not video files. No stock photography is included in this directory. No before/after relationship is inferred.
