I would build HairnThere around a retro mountain boutique meets modern luxury aesthetic. The Subaru logo concept gives you personality, adventure, and a little humor, while the website can feel polished and professional enough for clients booking $300+ color and extension appointments.

I'd use warm cream, espresso brown, dusty blush pink, and golden honey as your signature colors. Then make dark mode feel like a cozy, upscale mountain lodge at night.

The best part is that Harry can use the same brand colors across both modes, so the website feels cohesive rather than like two different businesses.

### Typography and website styling

I'd pair Playfair Display for elegant headings with Montserrat for navigation, service descriptions, and booking buttons.

For the homepage, I'd use the tagline “Great Hair Travels.” with two prominent booking options: Book a House Call and Book a Salon Appointment. That allows your business to grow beyond Suncrest without needing a rebrand.

My favorite detail would be a tiny animated Subaru traveling along a winding mountain road as someone scrolls through the website. It would give HairnThere a recognizable, playful signature without making the overall design feel childish.

Harry can build both modes using these same colors and let visitors switch between them with a sun/moon toggle.
